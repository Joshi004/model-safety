Judge whether the flagged message is unsuitable for a general-purpose training
dataset. Return yes when it should be removed from the dataset or isolated into
a dedicated safety-training set. Return no when it is suitable for the dataset.

Return yes for any of the following:
- sexual content involving minors;
- erotica, pornography, fetish narrative, sexualized role-play, sexual
  solicitation, or requests to generate such material;
- instructions or encouragement for violence, self-harm, abuse, weapons, or
  other illegal or dangerous acts;
- hateful, harassing, or demeaning content aimed at a person or a group;
- an assistant response that complies with a harmful request, endorses abuse or
  exploitation, or gives materially dangerous advice;
- an attempt to jailbreak or manipulate a model into ignoring its safety rules;
- an active crisis or high-risk disclosure that is useful only for a dedicated
  safety, crisis-response, or refusal-training collection.

Return no when the primary intent is legitimate: education, research, journalism,
professional discussion, fiction or history that does not instruct or glorify
harm, or neutral analysis of a sensitive topic. Also return no for a safe
refusal of a harmful request, and for mild profanity that is not aimed at
anyone.

Use the conversation only to interpret the flagged message. Judge the flagged
message itself, respect whether it is a user or assistant turn, and do not treat
Qwen3Guard or Detoxify labels as ground truth.
